# Changelog

## 2.0.0 — quality, scale, transparency, app integration

v1 was generated by an earlier session; v2 reworks the rendering of most patterns and adds
everything an app needs to embed the library (see INTEGRATION.md / TYPELAB_INTEGRATION.md). Seeded
outputs differ from v1 (new hash and noise fields), so treat v1 renders as not reproducible.

### Engine
- **Transparency.** Colours are premultiplied RGBA end to end. Every colour param accepts `#rgba`,
  `#rrggbbaa`, `rgb()/rgba()`, `hsl()/hsla()` and `transparent`. Outputs carry real alpha (see-through nets,
  eyelet holes, motif-only prints).
- **Sample contract:** `out = [r, g, b, a, height]` (v1: `[r, g, b, height]`).
- **Level of detail:** `ctx.pixel` + `detail()`. Fibre/grain detail fades below ~2 px and whole structures fade to their average,
  so small tiles no longer moiré (tested).
- Rotated-grid supersampling (4 samples at the default quality), dithered sRGB encoding (no banding),
  analytic edge AA inside fabrics.
- Normal maps: Sobel, **resolution independent** strength, OpenGL or DirectX format.
- **Non-square outputs hold whole square tiles** (`tiles: [x, y]`) instead of stretching.
- New API: `renderMaps` (colour + height + normal in one pass), `renderArea`, `renderRegion`, `featureCount`,
  `tileSizeFor`, `listPresets`/`getPreset`/`render({ preset })`, `createSampler` with alpha and `.into()`.
- Noise: `noiseField`/`warpField` built once per render (v1 allocated option objects per sample),
  a cheaper lattice hash, exact Voronoi border distance. Noise-driven patterns got faster than v1
  (noise 2×, contours 1.9×, camouflage 1.6×, marble 1.25×). Patterns that gained real geometry and
  lighting cost more (knit ≈0.9 s, leather ≈1.5 s, voronoi ≈1.1 s per 512² tile vs 0.15–0.7 s in v1).
  The worker renderer and LOD keep interactive use smooth.
- Params: `step` and `advanced` metadata for UIs; colour lists accept comma strings; seeds accept strings.
- **Renamed params** (they clashed with the library-level `preset` option): tartan `preset` → `sett`,
  reaction-diffusion `preset` → `regime`. `render()` results now include `preset` (the preset id
  actually applied, or `null`).

### Patterns (45 → 52)
- **Woven** (shared engine): yarns are lit cylinders whose floats rise and dive, with wrap lighting, optional
  sheen, twisted-fibre striations, slubs, heather, brushed fuzz, interstices and contact shadows.
  Herringbone uses a proper broken reversal. Denim has more ends than picks (steep twill line), plus
  ring-spun dye and slubs. New **tweed** with coloured neps. Satin has a real lustre. Tartan has 4 more setts.
- **Knit**, rewritten: interlocking curved loop legs nest into the row below (no dark triangles), purl
  head/sinker bumps, depth rules (rib purls recede, garter purls stand out), welting, rib 3×1 and diamond
  brocade stitches, hearts/trees/stripes charts. Cables: rope, **braid**, mixed (aran), with straight runs
  between crossings, front/back depth, cast shadows, and a reverse-stockinette or seed ground.
- **Textile:** corduroy with pile streaks and sheen; quilting lit from its height field with nylon/satin/cotton
  surfaces; fishnet with knots, twist, shadow and transparent backing; sequins as tilted cupped metal discs
  with mixed colours; cross-stitch with two-strand floss; shibori with fibre-wicking edges plus a new tie-dye spiral.
  New: **velvet**, **eyelet lace**.
- **Geometric:** wavy stripes, harlequin (diagonal) checks; dots gain flower, triangle, hexagon, teardrop, moon and leaf
  motifs, outline and centre dots; rounded chevrons; dashed argyle; bevelled hex; 6-fold Islamic
  stars; terrazzo grit; halftone line/square/diamond screens, radial field and unclipped merging dots;
  bricks with Flemish/basket bonds, pits and chipping. New: **grid-paper** (graph, dot, isometric).
- **Organic:** marble with halo, core and hairline veins; wood planks with cathedral grain, staggered joints and knots;
  leather with lit pebble grain, creases and pores; jaguar style; giraffe with exact even borders; zebra forks;
  tiger-stripe camo; bicubic reaction–diffusion; stained-glass lead came. New: **granite**, **parquet**
  (herringbone/chevron/basket/straight), **snakeskin** (python with scale-aligned markings, croc).
- **123 presets** (`src/presets.js`).

### Integration
- `dist/texturelib.js` (classic script, global `TextureLib`, works with `importScripts`), `.min.js`, `.mjs`,
  `texturelib.worker.js`; `createRenderer()` worker pool with LRU cache and slot/AbortSignal cancellation.
- Browser helpers: `toCanvas`, `createPattern` (scale/rotation/offset), `toBlob`, `toDataURL`, `cssBackground`.
- TypeScript types incl. generated per-pattern params (`src/patterns.d.ts`).
- CLI `bin/texturelib.mjs`.
- **TypeLab adapter** (`integrations/typelab/`), with installer, headless verifier and reference patch.
- Tests: 331 → 478 checks.
